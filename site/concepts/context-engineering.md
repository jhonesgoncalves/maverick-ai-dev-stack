# Context engineering

## What
Context is the minimum relevant architecture, paths, conventions, rules, constraints, and commands.

## Why
Useful context reduces guessing; excess context creates noise and exposure risk.

## When
Prepare it after the brief and revise it when the task changes.

## Example
See `templates/context-pack.md`.

## Common mistake
Pasting an entire repository or sensitive data into an agent.

## Checklist
- [ ] Paths are ordered
- [ ] Do-not-touch boundaries are named
