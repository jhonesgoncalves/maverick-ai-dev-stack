# Human in the loop

## What
The human owns authorization, design trade-offs, execution permissions, review, and merging.

## Why
AI proposes; it does not assume accountability.

## When
Always, especially around data, security, cost, and public contracts.

## Example
Require an explicit human decision in `REVIEW.md`.

## Common mistake
Giving an agent broad permissions because a task appears routine.

## Checklist
- [ ] Permissions are least-privilege
- [ ] Output is independently reviewed
