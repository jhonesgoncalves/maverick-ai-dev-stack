# Review

## What
Review checks scope, behavior, tests, security, reliability, maintainability, and the merge decision.

## Why
Generated output is not trustworthy merely because it is plausible.

## When
Use a reviewer after verification, ideally with a fresh perspective.

## Example
See `templates/review.md`.

## Common mistake
Reviewing the agent explanation instead of the diff and evidence.

## Checklist
- [ ] Scope matches the brief
- [ ] A human can explain the change
