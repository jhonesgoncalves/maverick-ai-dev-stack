# Verification

## What
Verification is the evidence that the intended behavior works and constraints hold.

## Why
A green command alone may not prove the requested outcome.

## When
Run it before review and record it in the PR artefact.

## Example
Tests, lint, type checks, and a focused manual scenario.

## Common mistake
Weakening tests to make a generated change pass.

## Checklist
- [ ] Behavior and edge cases are covered
- [ ] Commands are recorded
