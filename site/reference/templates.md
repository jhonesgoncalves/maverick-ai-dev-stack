# Templates

Maverick ships TASK, CONTEXT, PLAN, REVIEW, PR, ADR, SPEC, RISK, policy, and CI templates. Customize copies in your project deliberately; do not add secrets or company data to reusable templates.

The toolkit may also write task evidence such as `EVIDENCE.md`, `CHECKPOINT.md`, `REVIEW-FINDINGS.md`, `SECURITY-REPORT.md`, `DEPENDENCY-REVIEW.md`, and `MERGE-READINESS.md`. These generated reports support, rather than replace, a human review.
