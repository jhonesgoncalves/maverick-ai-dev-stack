# Maverick AI — Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea
Maverick AI is a developer intelligence and AI engineering workspace. The identity should feel technical, precise, fast and premium — not playful, cyberpunk, or overloaded with AI clichés.

**Primary name:** Maverick AI  
**Short product name:** Maverick  
**Suggested descriptor:** AI-powered Developer Workspace  
**Suggested line:** Understand. Build. Debug. Ship.

## Visual direction
The palette is intentionally inspired by the black + orange visual language requested for the project. The logo itself is original to Maverick AI.

### Primary palette
- Brand Orange — `#FF6B00`
- Bright Orange — `#FF8A1A`
- Deep Orange — `#E85D00`
- Brand Black — `#050505`
- App Background — `#070707`
- Surface 1 — `#0E0E0E`
- Surface 2 — `#151515`
- Border — `#292929`
- Main Text — `#F7F7F5`
- Muted Text — `#9B9B96`

### Color usage
Aim for roughly 75–85% black/neutral surfaces, 10–20% white/gray information, and 5–10% orange accents. Orange identifies the brand, active states, primary actions and important highlights. Avoid making entire screens orange.

## Typography
Use fonts already available in the project when possible.

- UI / marketing: `Inter` or `Geist`
- Code / technical data: `JetBrains Mono` or `Geist Mono`
- Fallbacks are provided in the token files.

Do not bundle font binaries into the project unless their licenses and distribution are explicitly handled.

## Logo system
- `maverick-ai-logo-dark.svg`: preferred lockup for dark surfaces.
- `maverick-ai-logo-light.svg`: lockup for light surfaces.
- `maverick-ai-mark.svg`: standalone M mark for favicon, app icon, compact sidebar and avatar.
- Monochrome files: only for restricted-color contexts.
- `assets/reference/maverick-ai-approved-concept.png`: visual concept approved during exploration; use as a mood/reference, not as the source of vector geometry.

### Safe space
Keep free space around the logo at least equal to approximately 20% of the mark height.

### Minimum recommended sizes
- Full lockup: 160 px wide on screen.
- Mark only: 24 px minimum; 32 px or more preferred.

### Do not
- stretch or skew the mark;
- recolor it with unrelated blues/purples;
- add heavy neon glows;
- put the orange lockup over low-contrast orange/red imagery;
- recreate the wordmark with a decorative or sci-fi font;
- copy visual assets or proprietary iconography from another product.

## UI language
Maverick should look like a premium developer tool:
- dark-first;
- dense enough for technical work, but with strong hierarchy;
- thin neutral borders;
- orange focus ring and primary CTA;
- restrained gradients;
- subtle orange ambient glow only in hero/empty states;
- data and code take priority over decorative art;
- icons should be simple line icons;
- status colors keep their semantic meaning instead of being forced to orange.

## Product voice
Short, technical, confident and useful. Prefer verbs and clear system language.

Examples:
- `Ask Maverick`
- `Analyze impact`
- `Inspect trace`
- `Create task`
- `Run agent`
- `Open architecture`

Avoid hype-heavy labels such as “MAGICAL AI POWER”.
