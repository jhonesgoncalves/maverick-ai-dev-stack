# FAQ

## Do I need agents?
No. The method works with a single assistant and manual copy/paste.

## Do I need a spec for every typo?
No. Match process weight to risk. A tiny fix can use a short TASK.md.

## Should the AI review its own code?
It can provide a second pass, but that is not independent evidence. Human review remains necessary for meaningful changes.

## Can I paste my company repository into this kit?
The kit is local text. Whether you may send repository content to an AI provider depends on your company's policies and agreements.

## What is the main metric?
For engineering: a correct, reviewable change with acceptable risk. Token count and lines generated are supporting signals, not outcomes.
